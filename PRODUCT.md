# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Vite + React, confirmed for a lightweight responsive scrollytelling experience.

## Users

Parents who recognize the invisible repetition of toddler care and non-parent friends who need a vivid way to understand it. The responsive story must serve desktop case-study review and casual mobile reading equally.

## Product Purpose

"912 Days" is a P302 interactive data story that turns estimated totals from Big Simba's first 2.5 years into a funny, then quietly moving, account of how quickly early childhood passes.

## Positioning

Rather than presenting a generic parenting dashboard, the story compounds specific small repetitions - diapers, books, outings, sleep, and words - into one personal realization: these totals equal one kid in 912 days.

## Operating Context

Visitors progress through one responsive, scroll-driven narrative of seven scenes. Scroll progress is the primary interaction, supported only by the diaper chart, book stack, and then-to-now word control.

## Capabilities and Constraints

The story is limited to seven scenes and three named accent interactions. All data is invented and estimated; no client- or Slalom-specific information may be used. Personal photos and bracketed statistics remain placeholders through Phase 1.

## Brand Commitments

The title is "912 Days" and the subject is Big Simba. The voice is funny and irreverent on the surface, warmly emotional by the close. The brief binds the visual direction to a bold, curated crayon/sketchbook world with hand-drawn motion.

## Evidence on Hand

## Product Principles

- The cumulative story matters more than visual novelty.
- Every revealed total must feel specific to Big Simba's early life.
- Humor creates room for the emotional landing.
- Shared patterns keep the experience light, legible, and finishable.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use readable body type, adequate contrast, semantic controls, and a reduced-motion fallback. Do not let the hand-drawn aesthetic obstruct the story.
